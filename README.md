# AI Based Advocate Chat (Legal Connect Platform)

An integrated AI-powered legal platform designed to simplify access to justice by connecting users with verified legal help, managing cases, and offering intelligent legal reference tools.

---

## Overview
Legal processes can often be time-consuming, confusing, and hard to navigate. This project offers a unified platform that connects users, advocates, and administrators, incorporating AI-assisted search and case management features.

---

## Key Features

### User & Guest Modules
* **AI Chatbot Assistance:** Quick query resolution and automated lawyer matching based on specific legal needs.
* **Advocate Directory:** Browse district-wide lawyer profiles, ratings, win rates, and fee transparency.
* **Appointment Scheduling & Chat:** Direct real-time communication and booking with assigned legal professionals.

### Advocate & Admin Modules
* **Case Management:** Secure registration, profile management, fee setup, and status tracking for assigned cases.
* **BNS Section Reference:** Built-in legal clarity references and automated outcome prediction tools.

---

## Technical Specifications
* **Language:** Python
* **Framework:** Django
* **Database:** SQLite
* **Frontend:** HTML, JavaScript, CSS
* **AI/NLP Tools:** NLTK, spaCy, TensorFlow / custom Keras model
